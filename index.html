Superstore Retail Analytics

An end-to-end analysis of the Sample Superstore dataset using Python. The project combines customer segmentation, retention analysis, seasonality, Pareto analysis and product profitability to turn thousands of retail transactions into business recommendations.

Python Pandas NumPy Scikit-learn Matplotlib Seaborn RFM K-Means Cohort Analysis
9,994order line items
5,009orders analyzed
793customers
2014–2017analysis period

Key findings

Discounts can destroy margin.

Profit margin is about 30% at 0% discount and declines as discounts increase, becoming negative at 21%+ discount.

Revenue can hide product problems.

Tables and Bookcases generate meaningful revenue but are unprofitable overall in the dataset.

Revenue is concentrated.

8 of 17 sub-categories contribute roughly 80% of total revenue, giving the business a clear prioritization opportunity.

Business recommendations

  • Set a disciplined discount ceiling.
  • Review pricing and costs for loss-making categories.
  • Give high-value customers targeted VIP treatment.
  • Run win-back campaigns for at-risk customers.
  • Investigate the early cohort-retention drop.
